About the Hospital

Reliable Healthcare for You & Your Family

Located directly opposite Enam Medical College Hospital on Thana Road, Parboti Nagar, Savar, Nahar General Hospital is a certified private medical institution committed to ethical, compassionate, and community-focused healthcare.

Our facility includes advanced operation theatres, digital diagnostics, 4D ultrasound, day-care wards, and 24-hour emergency response — all under one roof.

Clinical Departments

Comprehensive Medical & Surgical Care

Modern medical technologies and dedicated healthcare professionals serving the community of Savar and surrounding areas.

General & Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive procedures ensuring smaller incisions, less scarring, and faster recovery.

  • Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder)
  • Laparoscopic Appendectomy
  • Hernia repair & Hydrocele
  • Breast tumour & Thyroid surgery
  • Emergency & Paediatric trauma operations

Gynecology & Obstetrics

Comprehensive maternal care from antenatal counselling to safe childbirth and postnatal recovery.

  • Painless & Safe Normal Delivery
  • Emergency & Elective C-Section
  • High-risk pregnancy management
  • Infertility workup & menstrual disorders
  • Antenatal ultrasonography checkups

Pediatrics & Neonatal Care

Dedicated child specialists managing infant illness, neonatal jaundice, infections, and vaccination guidance.

  • Newborn assessment & immediate care
  • Paediatric respiratory & fever care
  • Child nutrition & growth monitoring
  • Immunisation counselling
  • Emergency paediatric admission

Internal Medicine & Diabetes

Expert diagnosis and systematic management of chronic and acute adult conditions.

  • Hypertension & Cardiovascular assessment
  • Diabetes Mellitus management
  • Fever, Typhoid, Dengue & Viral care
  • Gastrointestinal & Liver disorders
  • Kidney function & Geriatric medicine

Pathology & Diagnostics

Fast, calibrated investigations with computerised analysers for accurate same-day clinical reports.

  • CBC, ESR & Blood Grouping
  • Liver, Kidney & Lipid profiles
  • Hormonal & Thyroid tests
  • Digital X-Ray & 12-lead ECG
  • 4D Color Doppler Ultrasonography

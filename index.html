Welcome to Nahar General Hospital

Reliable Healthcare for You & Your Family

Located right across from Enam Medical College Hospital on Thana Road in Parboti Nagar, Savar, Nahar General Hospital is a certified private medical institution dedicated to delivering dependable, ethical, and community-focused healthcare.

Our facility features an advanced minor and major operation theatre (OT), digital diagnostics, ultrasonic imaging, day care wards, and round-the-clock emergency medical response.

Clinical Departments

Comprehensive Medical & Surgical Care

Our hospital brings together modern medical technologies and dedicated healthcare professionals to serve the community of Savar and surrounding areas.

General & Laparoscopic Surgery

State-of-the-art minimally invasive surgeries ensuring smaller incisions, minimal scarring, and quick recovery.

  • Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder stone)
  • Laparoscopic Appendectomy
  • Hernia repair & Hydrocele
  • Breast tumor & Thyroid surgery
  • Emergency pediatric trauma operations

Gynecology & Obstetrics

Comprehensive maternal healthcare from antenatal counseling to safe childbirth and postnatal rehabilitation.

  • Painless & Safe Normal Delivery
  • Emergency & Elective Cesarean Section (C-Section)
  • High-risk pregnancy management
  • Infertility workup & menstrual disorder treatment
  • Antenatal regular ultrasonography checkups

Pediatrics & Neonatal Care

Dedicated child specialists managing infant illnesses, neonatal jaundice, pediatric infections, and vaccination guidance.

  • Newborn immediate assessment & care
  • Pediatric acute respiratory care
  • Child nutrition & growth monitoring
  • Routine immunizations counseling
  • Emergency pediatric admission

Internal Medicine & Diabetes

Expert diagnosis and systematic management of chronic and acute adult conditions.

  • Hypertension & Cardiovascular assessment
  • Diabetes Mellitus management & counseling
  • Fever, Typhoid, Dengue & Viral care
  • Gastrointestinal & Liver disorders
  • Kidney function & Geriatric medicine

Pathology & Diagnostic Center

Fast, calibrated diagnostic investigations equipped with computerized analyzers for accurate clinical reports.

  • Hematology & Complete Blood Count (CBC)
  • Biochemistry & Lipid, Liver, Renal profiles
  • Hormonal tests & Thyroid profiles
  • Digital X-Ray & 12-lead ECG
  • Ultrasonography (USG 4D color doppler)
